Golden Turmeric & Ginger Milk

You need

  • 1 cup milk (or oat / almond)
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 cm fresh ginger, grated
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • 1 tsp honey

How to make

  1. Warm the milk in a small pot.
  2. Whisk in turmeric, ginger, cinnamon and pepper.
  3. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  4. Strain, let cool a little, stir in honey.
Why black pepper? It helps your body absorb curcumin, the natural compound that gives turmeric its golden colour. Enjoy it warm in the evening.
