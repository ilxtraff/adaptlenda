## Post #1 (with post.png)

🍵 **Golden Turmeric & Ginger Milk: the cosy evening drink everyone asks about**

When the weather turns cold and your knees and fingers feel a little stiff, nothing beats a warm golden cup before bed. It takes 10 minutes, costs almost nothing and tastes like a hug in a mug 🧡

**You need (1 cup):**
• 1 cup milk (cow, oat or almond)
• 1 tsp ground turmeric
• 1 cm fresh ginger, grated (or ½ tsp ground)
• ¼ tsp cinnamon
• A pinch of black pepper
• 1 tsp honey

**How to make it:**
1️⃣ Warm the milk in a small pot over low heat.
2️⃣ Whisk in turmeric, ginger, cinnamon and black pepper.
3️⃣ Let it simmer gently for 5 minutes. Don't let it boil!
4️⃣ Strain into your favourite mug, let it cool a little, then stir in the honey.

💡 **Why black pepper?** It helps your body absorb curcumin, the natural compound that gives turmeric its golden colour. Ginger and cinnamon add warmth and a lovely spicy aroma.

✨ **Extra tip:** add ½ tsp of coconut oil or a few drops of vanilla for an even creamier taste.

⚠️ If you take blood thinners or other regular medication, check with your doctor before drinking turmeric every day.
